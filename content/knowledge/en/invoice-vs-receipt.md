The e-invoice and the e-receipt are different tax-authority systems, even if both look like a “sales paper”.

## E-invoice
Fits trade between businesses: sales to a company or body that needs a certified invoice for filing and deduction. You create, review, then send electronically.

## E-receipt
Fits sales to a consumer at the cashier or POS: amount, items, and a live or near-live submit to the system.

## What to choose with DTS
Many clients need both, depending on sales channels. [ESS](/products/ess) creates invoices and receipts in the browser. [ERS](/products/ers) is cashier receipts only. Signing or sealing runs through [ESA](/products/esa).

The final duty to use one document type follows your activity and authority rules. Sales can explain the technical path after you share activity data.
