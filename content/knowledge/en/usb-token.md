The signing token is a USB device (or similar approved media) that holds the signature or seal certificate. Without it, legal submit of invoices and receipts to the tax authority cannot complete.

DTS does not market the token as a separate campaign product. The working path: request the certificate from the [signature service](/services/signature), then run [ESA](/products/esa) on the PC so it can read the token and sign documents.

## What happens in practice

1. You send a signature or seal request with your details
2. The licensed issuer writes the certificate to the token, with DTS partners
3. You install ESA and plug in the token
4. ESS creates the document; ESA signs it before submit

## Watch-outs

Do not share the token PIN, and do not use it on an untrusted machine. If the token is lost, tell the signature team during business hours.
