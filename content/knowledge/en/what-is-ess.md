---

ESS is an electronic sales subscription. From it you create invoices and receipts, review them, send them to the Egyptian Tax Authority, and track their status in one place.

Master data is stored once: products, customers, and suppliers. You organize work by branch and POS device, and people sign in from any device according to their permissions.

## Signing

ESA complements ESS. It runs on your PC and signs or seals documents before submit. It is not a separate campaign from the ESS cycle.
