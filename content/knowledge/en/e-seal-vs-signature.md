The usual request for Egyptian companies on the tax system is a signature for the authorized person and a seal for the company as a legal entity. Both are digital certificates; they are used differently.

## E-signature

Fits the person allowed to sign: a manager, accountant, or delegate. The document shows a signature issued by that person.

## E-seal

Fits the company as an entity. It seals invoices, receipts, and papers issued in the organization’s name, not only one individual’s.

## How to request from DTS

On the service form choose signature, seal, or both. The team replies on the signature inbox Sunday–Thursday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
