## Is a receipt the same as an invoice?
No. They are different systems. See [invoice or receipt](/knowledge/invoice-vs-receipt).

## Do I need a cashier device?
If you sell at a point of sale you need a device or screen that issues receipts. ESS covers receipts in the browser; ERS is cashier receipts only.

## Is ESS enough without a signature?
ESS creates the document. Legal submit needs a certificate, a token, and ESA.
