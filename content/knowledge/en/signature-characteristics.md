A licensed e-signature is not a scanned handwriting image. It is a crypto operation that binds the signer to the document at a point in time.

## Core traits

- **Identity:** the certificate is issued after the signer’s data is checked by a licensed authority.
- **Integrity:** any edit after signing fails verification.
- **Non-repudiation:** the certificate holder cannot easily deny the signature if the private key stayed on the token.
- **Device binding:** the private key is not a normal copied file; it is used through the token and software such as ESA.


## What that means for invoicing

Before an invoice or receipt goes to the tax authority it must be signed or sealed with a valid certificate. ESS creates the document; ESA completes the legal step on the PC.

Keep the token PIN private and do not use it on an untrusted machine. If the token is lost, tell the signature team during business hours.
