## What is the difference between signature and seal?

A signature identifies the person. A seal identifies the company on the document. Many companies request both.

## Is a scanned handwriting image an e-signature?

No. A licensed signature is a certificate and a key on a token, with cryptographic checks.

## Where do I apply?

On the [service page](/services/signature) or the signature inbox listed in [How to reach DTS](/knowledge/contact-hours).

## What after I receive the token?

Install ESA, plug in the token, then sign what ESS issues before tax submit.

## Token lost or PIN forgotten?

Do not use an unofficial copy. Tell the signature team in business hours so the certificate can be stopped and reissued under the licensed authority’s rules.

## Is ESS enough on its own?

ESS creates invoices and receipts. Legal signing needs a certificate, a token, and ESA. See [What ESS is](/knowledge/what-is-ess).
