An e-seal is a certificate for the company as a legal entity. It is not the same as a person’s signature. Both can be requested on the same form if you need them.

## Steps

1. Open the [signature and seal form](/services/signature)
2. Enter name, phone, email, and company name
3. Choose **seal** or **both** if you also need the delegate’s signature
4. Send; the message reaches the signature inbox Sunday–Thursday, 9:00 AM–5:00 PM
5. After the certificate is on the token, install [ESA](/products/esa) and plug the token in
6. Use the seal with what [ESS](/products/ess) issues


## Prepare before you send

Company and delegate data must match what will appear on the certificate. A mismatch delays issuance. We do not list a competitor’s branches; coordination runs through the DTS team and licensed partners.
