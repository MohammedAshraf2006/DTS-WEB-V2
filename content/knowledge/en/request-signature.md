Start on the [signature and seal service page](/services/signature). You fill the form, and the message reaches the signature inbox: tawqe3y_10thoframdan@dts-eg.com

The team replies during business hours: Sunday–Thursday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

## What to prepare

- Name, phone, and email
- Company name, if you have one
- Request type: signature, seal, or both

## After you send

The message goes to the signature inbox. If the site cannot send it, your mail app opens so you can finish the message yourself.

After the certificate is on the token, [ESA](/products/esa) signs what [ESS](/products/ess) issues.
