description: How to ask DTS for an e-signature or e-seal and connect it to your documents.
---

Request an e-signature or e-seal from the service page. The team replies on the signature inbox during business hours: Sunday–Thursday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

## What to prepare

- Name, phone, and email
- Company name, if you have one
- Request type: signature, seal, or both

## After you send

The message goes to the signature inbox. If the site cannot send it, your mail app opens so you can finish the message yourself.

[Open the request form](/services/signature)
