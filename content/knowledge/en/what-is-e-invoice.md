An e-invoice on the tax authority system is a digital document with a number and a status after submit: accepted, rejected, or in process. It is not a photographed paper invoice uploaded as a file.

From [ESS](/products/ess) you create the invoice, review it, send it, and track status with items, customers, and branches. Legal submit needs a signature or seal through [ESA](/products/esa) and the token.

If you sell to a consumer at the cashier you may need the receipt instead of the invoice, or both. See [invoice or receipt](/knowledge/invoice-vs-receipt).
