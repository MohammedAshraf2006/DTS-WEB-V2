The working DTS path for e-receipts:

1. Talk to sales to see if you need receipts only, or invoices and receipts
2. Subscribe to [ESS](/products/ess), or to [ERS](/products/ers) if work is cashier-only
3. Enter items, prices, POS devices, and users
4. Request a [signature or seal](/services/signature) if the certificate is not already on the token
5. Install [ESA](/products/esa) on the signing PC and plug in the token
6. Issue the receipt in the system and track its status after tax submit

Without a certificate, token, and ESA, legal submit does not complete even if the receipt appears on screen.
