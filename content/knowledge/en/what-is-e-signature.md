An electronic signature is a licensed digital certificate. It proves who signed an electronic document using public-key cryptography (a public and private key pair), so others can check that the file came from that person and was not changed after signing.

In Egypt the path sits on the e-signature law, the regulatory root, and licensed issuers. DTS helps you request the certificate, run it on your PC with [ESA](/products/esa), and connect what you sign to invoices and receipts in [ESS](/products/ess).

## What it covers

- A signature certificate for the authorized person
- An e-seal for the company as a legal entity
- The token that holds the certificate
- Links to government portals, above all the Egyptian Tax Authority


The working request starts on the [signature and seal page](/services/signature). DTS does not list another vendor’s branches; the path is the form, the certificate on the token, then ESA.
