An e-receipt is a sales document issued at the cashier and sent to the Egyptian Tax Authority’s e-receipt system. It fits businesses that sell to a final consumer more than a company-to-company invoice cycle.

At DTS the receipt is created in [ESS](/products/ess) or, for cashier-only work, in [ERS](/products/ers). Legal submit still needs a signature or seal certificate on a token, and [ESA](/products/esa) on the PC.

Who must join the system is set by tax authority decisions by activity and size. This page explains the DTS working path; it is not legal advice.
