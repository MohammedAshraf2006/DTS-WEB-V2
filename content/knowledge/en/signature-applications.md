The daily use for DTS clients is signing or sealing what ESS issues before submit to the Egyptian Tax Authority: invoices, receipts, and papers tied to coding and status.

## Common areas

- The e-invoice and e-receipt systems
- Signing forms and requests on other government portals, according to the certificate
- Internal documents that need a proven signer without a paper cycle every time
- The company e-seal on outputs of the entity, not only one person’s signature


The application here is clear: create in [ESS](/products/ess) or issue at the cashier with [ERS](/products/ers) when needed, and sign with the token through [ESA](/products/esa). Without the certificate, legal submit does not complete even if the document was created on screen.
